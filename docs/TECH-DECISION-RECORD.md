## Decision review rules

- A `PROPOSED` recommendation becomes `APPROVED` only through the appropriate project/client decision process.
- Provider-bound records remain `UNKNOWN`/`CLIENT DECISION REQUIRED` until account/provider/SOP information is supplied.
- A decision with high reversal cost requires proof-of-concept, staging, security, migration, operational, and rollback review before implementation.
- The technical decision record must be updated when Phase 4 validates data architecture or when a client decision resolves a dependency.
