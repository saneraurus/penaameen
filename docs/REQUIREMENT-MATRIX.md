# PENA AMEEN Requirement Traceability Matrix

**Phase:** 1 — Product Discovery
**Status:** Traceability baseline for the proposed product blueprint. A `CONFIRMED` requirement is confirmed by the phase brief/project evidence; a `PROPOSED` item still needs approval; a `BLOCKED` item depends on a client decision or missing evidence.

## Status and priority conventions

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Priority | `MUST HAVE`, `SHOULD HAVE`, `NICE TO HAVE`, `CLIENT DECISION REQUIRED`, or `OUT OF SCOPE`. |
| Status | Uses the project status system: `CONFIRMED`, `PROPOSED`, or `BLOCKED`. `BLOCKED` items must not be silently implemented as assumptions. |
| Migration impact | `Critical`, `High`, `Medium`, `Conditional`, or `None`; it identifies migration/launch sensitivity, not technical complexity. |
