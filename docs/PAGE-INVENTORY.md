# PENA AMEEN Page / Screen Inventory

**Phase:** 1 — Product Discovery
**Status:** PROPOSED inventory. It specifies required experiences and dependencies, not final routes, wireframes, or UI designs.
**Page count:** The inventory intentionally follows the required journeys and operations rather than a preset number of pages.

## Reading the inventory

- **SEO:** Critical / High / Medium / Low / N/A reflects product and migration importance, not a promise of current indexability.
- **Auth:** `No`, `Customer if enabled`, `Staff`, or `Conditional`.
- **Commerce:** `Yes` means it directly supports browse/cart/checkout/order/fulfillment; `Context` means it supports commerce discovery; `No` means it does not.
- **Admin dependency:** identifies the staff-managed data/workflow that must exist for the page to be accurate.
- **Migration dependency:** identifies source information or URL treatment needed before launch.
- **Status:** `MUST HAVE`, `SHOULD HAVE`, `NICE TO HAVE`, or `CLIENT DECISION REQUIRED` describes scope, not implementation readiness.

### Major page-requirement mapping

The detail rows below are grouped in the requirement matrix as: `REQ-PAG-001` Home; `REQ-PAG-002` Shop/category; `REQ-PAG-003` Product detail; `REQ-PAG-004` Search; `REQ-PAG-005` AL-BARQY/ACM education; `REQ-PAG-006` Article/archive; `REQ-PAG-007` Branches; `REQ-PAG-008` Events/gallery; `REQ-PAG-009` About; `REQ-PAG-010` Contact; `REQ-PAG-011` FAQ; `REQ-PAG-012` Legal/policy; `REQ-PAG-013` Cart; `REQ-PAG-014` Checkout; `REQ-PAG-015` Payment/order outcome; `REQ-PAG-016` Tracking; `REQ-PAG-017` Account; `REQ-PAG-018` Admin; and `REQ-PAG-019` System recovery. Individual page IDs below provide the screen-level traceability and purpose.

## Inventory coverage and controls

- The inventory includes the required public, commerce, customer, administrative, and recovery experiences for the MVP loop.
- A page may be implemented as a state, modal, or step rather than a distinct URL. That is a later interaction/design decision.
- `CLIENT DECISION REQUIRED` pages may not be silently omitted if their source URLs are indexed; their migration treatment must still be decided.
- The final source-to-target URL list, precise page count, final labels, and routing are Phase 2/migration work after data and SEO review.
